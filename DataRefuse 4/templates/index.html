03 / LOCATION DATA AUDIT

Review which apps can access your location.

A guided checklist. DataRefuse cannot inspect your phone directly or read live GPS permissions.

On iPhone
  1. Open Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. Tap Location Services.
  3. Review each app's access: Never, Ask Next Time, While Using, or Always.
  4. Change permissions you do not need.
On Android
  1. Open Settings → Location → App location permissions.
  2. Review which apps have location access.
  3. Check precise vs approximate location where available.
  4. Change access to Don't allow or only while using, where suitable.
Important about the information here

App profiles are preliminary research guides, not claims that every listed data type is collected in every situation. Practices depend on features, settings, jurisdiction and policy updates. Follow the official policy link and verify before relying on a profile. Alternatives are not certified as data-free.

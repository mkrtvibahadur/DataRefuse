# DataRefuse design system

## Design direction
Editorial archive / public-interest research desk. Dark charcoal canvas, document-like evidence panels, restrained accent colours and generous typographic hierarchy. Keep forms and controls clear and accessible; do not sacrifice usability for visual effects.

## Typography
- Display: DM Serif Display, 400; use large scale, tight leading (0.98–1.1) and slightly negative tracking.
- Body/UI: DM Sans, 400–700; body line-height 1.6–1.7.
- Metadata: DM Mono, 400–500; 10–12px, uppercase, letter spacing 0.08–0.22em.

## Colour tokens
- Page background: `#111111`
- Header/footer: `#090909` / `#080808`
- Surface: `#1B1B1B`
- Secondary surface: `#222222`
- Paper panel: `#F1EBDD`
- Primary text: `#F5F1E8`
- Muted text: `#AAA59B`
- Border: `#3A3936`
- Primary accent: `#D9B94F` (warm yellow)
- Secondary accent: `#244BDB` (blue)
- Occasional highlight: `#D95B66` (red)

## Hierarchy
- H1: clamp(48px, 7vw, 92px), DM Serif Display, 400, line-height 0.98.
- Hero H1: clamp(64px, 10vw, 128px), DM Serif Display, 400.
- H2: clamp(29px, 4vw, 46px), DM Serif Display, 400.
- H3: 19–26px; DM Sans or DM Serif Display depending on context.
- Body: 16px DM Sans, line-height 1.65.
- Caption/eyebrow: 10–11px DM Mono, uppercase, tracked.

## Layout and surfaces
- Main max width: 1180px; 90vw on desktop.
- Navigation: 76px high, black, thin bottom border.
- Section rhythm: 44–76px vertical spacing; panel padding 22–30px.
- Grid: two-column layouts on desktop, single-column on mobile; app directory uses two columns.
- Cards/panels: square corners, thin borders, flat surfaces; no generic pill cards or heavy shadows.
- Evidence excerpts: warm paper background, dark serif text, blue left rule.
- Responsive breakpoint: 760px.

## Content voice
Use active, direct sentences. Avoid unnecessary hedging such as “may be involved”, “options to consider” and “may help”. Keep factual caveats where they prevent unsupported claims: app profiles are prompts to verify, alternatives are not certified as data-free, keyword matches are not legal conclusions, and generated requests are not sent automatically.
